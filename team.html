Our Team

Meet The Heart Of Sapphire GP

At Sapphire GP, our team is more than just a group of healthcare professionals—we're a family dedicated to your wellbeing. Each member brings unique skills, passion, and years of experience to ensure you receive personalised, compassionate care at every visit. Whether you're here for a routine check-up, expert skin treatments, or ongoing health support, you can trust our warm and welcoming team to put your health first. Discover the difference genuine care can make at Sapphire GP.

Dr Sanoubar Keshavarzi
General Practitioner, Female,
FRACGP, MD, PGDPH

Dr Sanoubar Keshavarzi

Dr Sanoubar Keshavarzi is a highly experienced General Practitioner and a Fellow of the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners. She has been practising since 1994 and provides comprehensive GP care with a focus on holistic and preventative health.

Dr Keshavarzi holds certifications in Women's Reproductive Health, Sexual Health, and Family Planning, along with a diploma in Public Health. She has a special interest in Women's Health and Mental Health, offering services including cervical screening, implanon and Mirena insertion and removal, and broad care in children's and mental health.

Dr Keshavarzi is currently welcoming new patients.

Dr Reza Meschi
General Practitioner, Male, FRACGP,
MD, Skin Cancer Surgery & Laser,
Aesthetic Medicine

Dr Reza Meschi

Dr Reza Meschi is an experienced General Practitioner who has been practicing since 1995. He is a Fellow of the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners and a member of the Australian College of Aesthetic Medicine.

Dr Meschi has extensive expertise in skin cancer management and surgery, including advanced techniques such as skin flaps and grafts. He holds Postgraduate Diplomas in Skin Cancer Surgery and in Skin Laser & Cosmetic Procedures. He stays up to date with the latest diagnostic and treatment methods to provide optimal care.

Dr Meschi is currently welcoming new GP patients. His clinical interests include skin cancer care, laser skin tightening, cosmetic injectables, and minor surgical procedures.

Tannaz
Practice Nurse, Female, BSc

Tannaz

Tannaz is a highly skilled Registered Nurse with a Bachelor of Nursing, known for her compassionate approach and dedication to patient care. With extensive experience in both general nursing and the field of cosmetic medicine, she has developed particular expertise in skin care and laser skin treatments. Tannaz is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, radiant skin, and stays current with the latest advances in laser technology and dermatological care.

In addition to her broad nursing background, Tannaz offers a range of cosmetic and therapeutic skin procedures, working closely with clients to understand their individual needs and develop tailored treatment plans. Her gentle, professional manner puts patients at ease, ensuring they feel comfortable and confident throughout their care.

Tannaz is committed to ongoing professional development and believes in providing evidence-based, high-quality services. She values building lasting relationships with her patients, supporting them at every stage of their health and skincare journey.
