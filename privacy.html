Billing Policy

At Sapphire General Practice, we are a mixed billing clinic. This means that while some consultations and patients are bulk billed, others may incur a private fee. Your billing type is determined at the discretion of the treating doctor and depends on the nature and length of your consultation.

If you are privately billed, we will process your Medicare rebate at the end of your visit, making it easier for you to claim your entitled refund.

Bulk Billing Eligibility

The Following Patients Are Bulk Billed At Our Practice:

  • Children under the age of 16
  • Mental health consultations
  • GP Management Plans (Care Plans)
  • Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander patients (under the CTG program)
  • Some pension card holders (as determined by the doctor)
