Welcome to Sapphire General Practice

At Sapphire GP, our team has provided care across the NSW East Coast for the past decade and our mission is to provide the highest standard of care to our community. We are a General Practice with 2 GP Fellows who have a special interest in a range of concerns including Chronic Care Management, Women’s Health, Skin Cancer Management, Childrens Health and Mental Health.

Sapphire General Practice is a MIXED BILLING GP in Wyoming NSW with Bulk Billing options available for children, mental health appointments, some pensioners and other patients to the discretion of the doctor. Non-rebatable fees apply for all clinical procedures.

Conveniently Located

Sapphire GP is conveniently located on Sapphire Street in Wyoming. We proudly serve individuals and families across Wyoming and nearby suburbs including Gosford, Woy Woy, Narara, Lisarow, and Tumbi Umbi. With local parking and easy access, visiting your trusted local doctors is simple and stress-free.

Opening Hours

The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on weekends and Public Holidays. Our friendly team provides professional care in a warm, welcoming environment — ensuring your health needs are met during the week with compassion, attention, and commitment to your wellbeing.

How to Book

Appointments can be booked by calling (02) 4341 7722 or by clicking the “Book Appointment” button on our website. Online bookings are made through HotDoc. We offer flexible options so you can schedule your visit easily, whether by phone or online—whatever works best for your time and comfort.

After Hours Care

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call 000 immediately. For urgent care outside of normal clinic hours, contact the National Home Doctor Service on 13SICK (13 7425). After-hours doctors are available to visit your home and provide medical care when your regular GP clinic is closed.

Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander Health

Our dedicated health team is committed to providing safe health checks and ongoing support for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people of all ages. We aim to ensure everyone receives the care they need, respecting their unique cultural backgrounds. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where community members feel valued as they access essential health services.

We acknowledge the Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Traditional Custodians of the land on which we work, live and dream. We pay respect to Elders past, present and emerging.

  • Annual health checks
  • Health care planning
  • Support to manage health
  • Connection to other services
  • Outreach visits when needed
