Health Checks & Comprehensive Health Assessments

The true value of our Health Assessments lies in their proactive, preventative nature. Supported by the latest medical research, scheduling an assessment is the best way to safeguard your future well-being. It is recommended that you undergo an assessment if you are 75 or older, or between 45 to 49 years old.

Our Health Assessments are structured into three key steps: Review of Medical History, Comprehensive Examination, and Clear Recommendations.

If you are aged 75 or over, we recommend an annual comprehensive health assessment, bulk-billed under Medicare.

The assessment of 45–49 year olds is designed to prevent chronic diseases:

  • Early detection helps identify potential health concerns before symptoms develop.
  • Personalized lifestyle advice empowers you to make informed decisions about your health.
  • Comprehensive monitoring over time provides vital tracking of cardiovascular and metabolic markers.

The Three Steps of a Health Assessment:

Step 1: Review of Medical History
  • Detailed lifestyle review, including physical activity, nutrition, and sleep hygiene.
  • Evaluation of family health history to identify potential predispositions to heart disease, stroke, or diabetes.
  • Comprehensive review of all current prescription medications, over-the-counter supplements, and immunisation status.
Step 2: Comprehensive Clinical Examination
  • Routine vital signs check: blood pressure, resting pulse, and oxygen saturation.
  • Physical health screening: height, weight, Body Mass Index (BMI), and waist circumference.
  • Cardiovascular and respiratory evaluation, along with joint mobility, vision, and hearing checks.
  • Pathology investigations including fasting blood glucose, full lipid profile, kidney and liver function, and urinalysis.
Step 3: Clear Findings & Practical Recommendations

Following your assessment, your GP will discuss the findings in detail and formulate a personalized Health Management Plan. This may include lifestyle modifications, nutritional guidance, medication adjustments, or referrals to allied health specialists (e.g., dietitians, physiotherapists) under Medicare-supported Team Care Arrangements.

Medicare Eligibility & Bulk Billing:

Health Assessments for patients aged 75 and over, and eligible patients aged 45 to 49 with chronic disease risk factors, are bulk-billed under Medicare with no out-of-pocket costs.
