Expert Care with a Personal Touch.

We believe every patient deserves to feel heard, respected, and supported—no matter their stage of life or health needs

Our team consists of both female and male doctors, allowing you to choose the GP you feel most comfortable with. Our female GP specialises in women’s health and reproductive health, ensuring tailored care for all aspects of women’s wellbeing. We also offer a broad range of general practice services to support individuals and families in our community.

Conveniently located at 6 Sapphire Street, Wyoming, just off the Pacific Highway, our clinic offers on-site parking for patients, including accessible parking spaces and wheelchair ramps for easy access.

We are proud to welcome new patients, and walk-in appointments are accepted whenever possible. From the moment you are greeted by our friendly reception team to your consultation with one of our approachable GPs or our experienced practice nurse, we strive to make every visit comfortable, caring, and supportive.

Choose Sapphire General Practice for high-quality, personalised medical care, right in the heart of your community.

Accessibility.

Sapphire GP is wheelchair accessible and designed to accommodate patients of all ages and abilities. Please let us know if you need any special assistance when visiting.
